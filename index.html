WOD Tracker tracking test

Can your phone track a whole workout?

This test checks one thing before we build the app: whether body tracking runs fast enough, for long enough, on your iPhone. Nothing is analyzed yet. You'll send the results to Claude at the end.

Filming checklist
  • Phone propped up and still, sideways (landscape).
  • Your whole body, the bar, the rope area and any machine in frame.
  • Side-on or at 45°, so the rig doesn't hide you.
  • Plug the phone in if you can. The screen stays on during the test.
  • Don't switch apps: tracking pauses while you're away.

Tip: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, to open this like an app.
